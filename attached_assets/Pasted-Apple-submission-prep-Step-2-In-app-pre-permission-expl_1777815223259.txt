Apple submission prep — Step 2: In-app pre-permission explanation screens.

Goal:
Before iOS system permission prompts appear, StillHere should explain clearly why each permission is needed. This improves Apple review trust and user acceptance.

Do not change permission logic unless needed to show the explanation before the prompt.

Build/verify pre-permission education for:

1. Location
2. Notifications
3. Motion sensors

---

LOCATION SCREEN

Title:
Location helps your Safety Circle find you when it matters

Body:
StillHere uses location for safety features like SOS, Safe Walk, driving safety, check-ins, and live maps.

Your location is only shared with people you choose. You can pause or change sharing anytime.

Button:
Continue

Secondary text:
You stay in control.

---

NOTIFICATIONS SCREEN

Title:
Notifications keep your safety loop active

Body:
StillHere sends check-in reminders, safety alerts, all-clear updates, and urgent messages from your Safety Circle.

These alerts help the system reach you before escalating to your contacts.

Button:
Continue

Secondary text:
You can change notification settings anytime.

---

MOTION SCREEN

Title:
Motion helps detect serious events

Body:
StillHere can use motion sensors to help detect possible falls or vehicle crashes and start an emergency flow if you do not respond.

Button:
Continue

Secondary text:
Motion data is used for safety, not surveillance.

---

REQUIREMENTS

1. Show these screens before triggering the OS permission prompt.
2. Keep wording calm and non-alarming.
3. Use StillHere’s existing design style and colors.
4. Do not use fear language.
5. Do not block the entire app forever if the user declines.
6. If permission is denied, show a gentle degraded-protection message:
   “StillHere will still work, but some safety features may be limited.”
7. Add route or onboarding step only if one does not already exist.
8. If existing permission education screens already exist, update their copy instead of rebuilding.
