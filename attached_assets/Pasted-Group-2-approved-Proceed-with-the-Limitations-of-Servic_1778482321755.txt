  What StillHere is, and what it isn't

  Before you finish setting up, please read this carefully.

  StillHere is a safety check-in app. It helps your Safety Circle notice when you miss a check-in or send an SOS, so they can
  decide what to do.

  StillHere is not an emergency service. We do not contact 911, 999, 112, 000, or any local emergency number for you. We do not
  dispatch police, ambulance, or rescue services.

  StillHere is not a medical device. Fall sensing and possible-crash alerts use your phone and watch sensors. They are best-effort
  and may not detect every event. They are not a replacement for a medical alert pendant or hospital monitoring.

  Alerts are best-effort. When StillHere starts an alert flow, we attempt to reach your contacts through available channels such as
  push notifications, SMS, email, or phone calls, depending on what is enabled. Delivery depends on:

  Your phone having signal, battery, and permissions enabled
  Your contacts' phones being on and reachable
  Carrier networks and third-party services such as Apple, Google, and Twilio
  We cannot guarantee that every alert will be received, or how quickly.

  If you are in immediate danger and can safely do so, call your local emergency number first. StillHere is a layer on top of, not
  a replacement for, calling for help yourself.

  By tapping Continue, you confirm you understand the limits of this service.
